The port removes TypeScript types, isolates the five CosyVoice3 graphs, uses local ONNX Runtime Web, and rejects empty output or hitting the token limit. The RPC worker handles browser-only asset loading, provider selection, PCM transfer and lifecycle. The LibriTTS-R speaker 4446 preset was derived by Danyal Malik from utterance 4446_2275_000002_000009, LibriTTS-R by Koizumi et al. 2023, CC BY 4.0. The quantized graphs derive from Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-2512, Apache-2.0.
