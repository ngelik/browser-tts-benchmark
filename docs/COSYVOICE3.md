# Fun-CosyVoice3 0.5B (2512)

The benchmark runs FunAudioLLM's December 2025 checkpoint through Danyal Malik's browser ONNX export. The five model graphs total **905,670,668 bytes**. They use Q8 for the autoregressive language model and flow steps, Q4 for embeddings and the flow encoder, and mixed Q4/int8 for HiFT. The export includes a fixed initial flow-noise table, tokenizer and one precomputed English reference voice. [Original checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/FunAudioLLM/Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-2512/tree/29e01c4e8d000f4bcd70751be16fa94bf3d85a18) · [Pinned browser export](https://huggingface.co/danyalmalik/Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-ONNX-WebGPU/tree/dcdb9df4bce4a47d318f5f8736de0b080efd944f).

The canonical English article is passed to the Quick Inference frontend one source span at a time. The adaptation preserves comma-grouped amounts and scaled currency such as `$1.8 million`. Its English number/date normalization and Qwen2 byte-level BPE produce 93 internal utterances from 93 source spans, with at most 62 BPE tokens in an utterance. Both execution providers allow four WASM threads. The worker applies upstream repetition-aware sampling (top-p 0.8, top-k 25, window 10), ten cosine-schedule Euler steps with guidance 0.7, and HiFT at 24 kHz. Sampling and vocoder noise use the upstream unseeded RNG. A missing stop token at the length limit raises an error. The first-PCM event is emitted when HiFT returns the first completed utterance's float samples. [Pinned implementation](https://github.com/mdanyalmalik/quick-inf/blob/b50b3b8576f486d55fec5c366a0d8305a1fe8c41/src/runtime/speech/speech-engine.ts).

The original model covers nine languages (Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, Spanish, French, Italian and Russian), along with regional Chinese dialects. The browser export supplies one preset, derived from LibriTTS-R speaker 4446. The checkpoint also supports conditioning on arbitrary voices. Model and exported graph terms are Apache-2.0. The preset features derive from LibriTTS-R by Koizumi et al. under CC BY 4.0. The adapted Quick Inference code carries PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0.
