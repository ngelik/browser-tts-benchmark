# Community conduct

Keep discussion respectful, specific and focused on reproducible evidence. People
with different backgrounds and experience levels are welcome. Explain technical
criticism without personal attacks, and correct mistakes openly.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, impersonation, publishing private information
and deliberately falsifying benchmark evidence are not acceptable. Do not treat a
slow model, a failed experiment or an unsupported device as a contributor failure.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close misleading submissions, or
restrict participation when needed to protect the community. For conduct violations,
use GitHub's built-in reporting tools. Report sensitive software vulnerabilities
through the private channel in SECURITY.md; ordinary reproducibility questions
belong in an issue or pull request with private data removed.
